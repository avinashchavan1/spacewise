Spacewise Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Spacewise is a disk space analyzer for macOS. It is designed so that your information stays on your Mac.

What Spacewise reads

To show how your storage is used, Spacewise reads the names, sizes and modification dates of files and folders in the locations you choose to scan. To find duplicate files it reads file contents and computes checksums. All of this happens on your Mac.

What Spacewise collects

Nothing. Spacewise does not collect, store on a server, transmit or share any personal data or file information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What Spacewise stores on your Mac

Spacewise remembers the folders you scanned recently (as macOS bookmarks, so it can open them again) and your settings. This stays in the app's own storage on your Mac and is removed if you delete the app.

Changes to your files

Spacewise only moves or removes files when you ask it to. Removed items are moved to the Trash, except items that are already in the Trash.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/avinashchavan1/spacewise.
