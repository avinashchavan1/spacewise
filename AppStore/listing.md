## Description
Spacewise shows you exactly what is using space on your Mac and helps you get it back.

SEE EVERYTHING AT A GLANCE
• Scan your whole disk, your Home folder or any folder in seconds
• Explore folders as a treemap, where bigger blocks use more space
• Sort the largest files on your disk and filter by type or size
• See how much space videos, photos, apps, archives and code take

CLEAN UP WITH CONFIDENCE
• Smart suggestions: app caches, logs, Xcode build data, simulators, developer caches, old installers and old downloads
• Every suggestion is labelled Safe or Review, so you know what to check first
• Find exact duplicate files by comparing their contents, not just their names
• Collect items from anywhere into one cleanup list and review it before removing
• Everything you remove goes to the Trash, so you can always put it back

TIDY UP
• Sort a messy Downloads or Desktop folder into subfolders by type, month, year or extension
• Preview every move before anything changes, and undo with one click

FAST AND PRIVATE
• A multi-threaded scanner reads hundreds of thousands of files per second
• Built natively for macOS with SwiftUI, in light and dark mode
• No accounts, no tracking, no network access: your files never leave your Mac

## Review notes (App Review Information → Notes)
Spacewise analyzes disk usage. To test: click "Scan Home Folder" on the Overview screen and click Allow in the folder panel (the app is sandboxed and only reads folders the user chooses). Explore, Large Files, File Types, Duplicates and Cleanup then show results. Removing files always asks for confirmation and moves items to the Trash. Organize sorts the loose files of a chosen folder into subfolders and offers Undo.
